      About this guide

      What this is

      Leonida Ledger is a free, offline trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5. It covers all 55 trophies from the official list — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned. Your checklist lives in your browser's local storage on this device only; export it to a file to move it or back it up. The app loads nothing from the network and works fully offline.

      Sources

      Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official list exactly as PlayStation publishes them, cross-checked against the Exophase and PSNProfiles listings of the same list (the two agree on every name, description and grade). Counts the guides cite — 42 Shooting Range challenges, 250 stunts, 52 areas of Leonida, 9 gangs, 8 garage properties, 18 meditation spots, 14 drone-race locations, 100 WAiNK locations, 500 tracked choices — are the totals carried in that official list's own data. Everything else in the guide text is our own strategy writing, built from the official descriptions plus general Grand Theft Auto series knowledge, and is labelled as such wherever it appears. No text here is copied from any other guide.

      How the flags work

      Missable ⚑ flags are this guide's inferences from the official descriptions — a description that describes a single set-piece moment gets flagged, with the reasoning written out in that trophy's guide. Kind and phase are our own organisation of the list (story, collectible, activities, combat, exploration and so on), derived from what each description actually asks for. Spoiler shield hides the names and descriptions of unearned story trophies, the way PlayStation hides its 28 hidden trophies.
